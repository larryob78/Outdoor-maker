# Photographic mockup templates

Added 7 November 2026. The street poster, roadside billboard and retail screen are synthetic location photographs generated with OpenAI image generation for this project. They depict fictional placements. Each was generated at 1536 × 1024 with a blank chroma surface, which was keyed to a neutral backing for the shipped JPEG. The four inner display corners were calibrated individually.

The built-in scenes are safe for concept review as illustrations of context; they do not represent booked or verified media locations. Upload your own photograph to review an actual site. The original drawn presets remain selectable, and saved projects keep their prior corner placements. The review PDF uses the three photographic presets plus the uploaded scene if present.

The [generated originals](scene-assets/originals/) and [sample artwork composites](scene-assets/previews/) are archived here. Only the [calibrated JPEG templates](../public/scenes/) are included in the site build.
